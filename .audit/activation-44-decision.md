## Selection

One existing-Node CLI exposes `materialize --source <local-repo> --revision <full-sha> --store <absolute-store>` and `activate --package <absolute-version-directory> --project <absolute-project>`. No remote lookup, package manager, new dependency or mutable-HEAD activation. A direct-path-only design is smaller for existing trees but does not supply the requested installation and repeats two activation entries.

Activation verifies the installed payload and uses one package entry. No replacing a prior version, upgrading, deactivating or rewriting active attempt pins in this unit. Publication and settings replacement use staging/same-directory rename for sequential failure safety, not cross-process locking or crash-recovery guarantees.
