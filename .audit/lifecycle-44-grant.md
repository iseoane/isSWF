Process identity must not be inferred from a saved Markdown status or a bare PID. A missing/unknown owner blocks replacement. ## Selection rubric

- Small explicit interface and one clear owner of lifecycle metadata.
- Correct launch/completion/interruption states with conservative unknown handling.
- Survives loss of initiating chat without relying on pstack child teardown behavior.
- Reconciles crashes without duplicate writes or false success.
- Reuses current pinned-input and bounded-tool contracts without adding a new queue, framework or resolver.
- Rerunnable deterministic tests reproduce real process facts, distinguish model behavior and cover failures.

Serial gates are design selection, source freeze, independent verdict, integration and committed-source replay. Avoid repeated full-tree research or model retries used to manufacture PASS.
