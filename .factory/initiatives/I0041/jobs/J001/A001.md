## Options and recommendation

1. **More reminders/reload the entire skill per dispatch:** cheap but still depends on the coordinator remembering to do it; adds context and has the same bypass. Reject as sole repair.
2. **Mandatory existing template plus worker/reviewer checks:** useful semantic redundancy and readable ownership, but still instruction-level. Include it, without calling it mechanical enforcement.
3. **Small shared deterministic validator attached to native dispatch boundaries:** recommended structural component. Validate explicit selection/decline or a justified no-applicable-methods declaration, allowed forms/references, observed checks/not-run reasons and prohibitions. Do not infer which methods apply or choose them; those remain coordinator/reviewer judgment. Validate the pinned brief bytes actually handed to the child, not another editable mirror or a boolean “validated” claim.
4. **A new dispatcher/runtime, global state store or model judge on every call:** disproportionate and conflicts with native execution ownership. Not recommended.

Recommended combination: existing canonical brief + narrow native structural gate + worker acknowledgement/reviewer evidence check + fresh reconciliation at resume/compaction and functional-unit boundaries. Keep current actor, grant, checkout, candidate and remaining budgets in existing records; no separate policy truth store. Recover only a compact current-action reference after compaction, not an ever-growing copy of all methodology. Such reminders improve orientation; the dispatch gate is what removes reliance on remembering the structural prerequisite.
