## Blocking finding

### F001 — Assign the pending correction’s producer and serialized handoff

**Severity:** blocking  
**Origin:** introduced  
**Confidence:** high  
**Locations:**
- `.agents/roles/references/feature-delivery.md:23–27`
- `.factory/templates/correction-inbox.md:4`
- Affected consumers: `.agents/hosts/claude-code.md:61`, `.agents/hosts/pi.md:52`

The receiving delegated coordinator is explicitly responsible for creating and checkpointing the correction record. However, when the parent cannot deliver the correction through a supported live channel, the fallback requires a pending entry for that same coordinator to inspect later, without assigning its creation to the parent or defining how the trusted instruction reaches the record owner.

Concrete walkthrough:

1. The parent receives a trusted correction.
2. Parent→coordinator live messaging is unavailable.
3. The coordinator therefore has not received the correction.
4. The protocol nevertheless requires a pending record created by the coordinator.
5. Neither host guide nor template supplies the missing producer/handoff step.

The shared-checkout case adds an ordering constraint: the coordinator must checkpoint before relaying, but cannot write while its worker is active. Waiting for a safe handoff is correctly required; what remains unspecified is who retains the undelivered instruction and how it becomes the pending entry at that boundary.

**Impact:** the portable fallback is incomplete for its defining failure case. A caller must improvise ownership or may leave no entry for the mandatory pre-review inspection. Existing string assertions pass without resolving this gap.

**Smallest correction:** distinguish parent/sender from recipient coordinator, assign responsibility for retaining an undelivered correction, and define its safe serialized recording/handoff and recipient reconciliation. Preserve exclusive ownership, provenance, frozen inputs and budgets. No new transport, polling service, runtime enforcement or additional actor is needed.

Search across `.agents` for `correction|C001` found the canonical rule and both host summaries; none supplies the missing step. Treat these as one defect class and correct them together.
